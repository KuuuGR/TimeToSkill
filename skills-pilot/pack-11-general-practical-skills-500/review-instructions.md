# Review instructions — pack-11

1. Treat each record as a candidate, not an approved skill.
2. Preserve `candidate_id`, `source_id`, `source_item_id`, and `source_url`. Do not invent candidate IDs.
3. Check distinctness against existing pilot proposals and other packs. Do not automatically merge similar candidates.
4. Accept only consumer-relevant, teachable skills that can have a meaningful three-level progression. Avoid merely occupational duties, broad knowledge areas, vague traits, and near-duplicate variants.
5. If accepting a candidate, write an original skill name and description, exactly three levels, and observable completion criteria for each level. Each level must progress meaningfully; do not just increase a quantity without changing capability.
6. Include safety requirements when relevant. Do not provide unsafe medical, animal-handling, electrical, chemical, or tool instructions.
7. Use one emoji per proposal, with a brief rationale. Reuse is allowed but avoid unnecessary collisions across the catalog.
8. Source descriptions establish provenance, not necessarily consumer-level instructions. Do not infer facts that the source does not support. Where more evidence is needed, leave a named gap and source URL.
9. Keep a decision for every candidate: `accept`, `reject`, `defer`, or `needs_source_research`, with a concise reason. Do not silently drop records.
10. The output remains pilot proposals. Do not mark them production-ready or edit production catalogs.

# Technology, Science and Niche Interests — Editorial Pilot Review

## Scope

Reviewed all **114 candidate records** from `technology-science-niches` version 1 supplied in the archive. The review uses original candidate IDs, descriptions, URLs and the four registered source IDs. No source-candidate records were modified. This is an editorial review, not a production import.

## Results

- Drafted **30 skill proposals**, each with three observable progression levels and one topic-matched emoji.
- Candidate decision counts: `accepted_as_skill_basis` 17, `grouped_into_proposal` 47, `merge_as_inspiration` 3, `needs_additional_sources` 22, `not_a_distinct_skill` 6, `occupational_or_abstract` 13, `out_of_scope` 6.
- Distinct emoji among proposals: **30 / 30**. Unique emojis are a result of meaningful fit in this small pilot, not a requirement for the eventual 1001-skill catalogue.
- The review groups related transport spotting targets together, bird and butterfly observations together, band-specific DX hobbies together, and different model substrates/subjects where their underlying practice is shared.
- No candidate was deleted or rewritten in the supplied source database.

## Important editorial choices

- **Not every accepted source record is a separate skill.** Related source candidates may be grouped as supporting inspirations; these are identified through `source_candidate_ids`.
- **Not ready for approval:** amateur radio transmission, drone flying, mushroom hunting, amateur chemistry, beekeeping/insect keeping, magnet fishing, metal detecting, caving, mine exploration and tunnelling need additional safety, legal, welfare or evidence checks. Mine exploration and tunnelling are excluded from a self-directed consumer progression in this draft.
- Astrology and ghost hunting were not selected as skills in their current descriptions: the supplied records do not establish a suitable practical progression for this catalogue. A future, differently scoped skill such as critical evaluation of unusual claims would need its own suitable source basis.
- The Scout badge records provide titles but no requirements in this input. They were only treated as inspirations when a separate established concept in the package supported the proposed practice.
- Levels and descriptions are original editorial adaptations. Source descriptions are not represented as if they supplied the three-level progression.

## Supplementary research

`additional-sources.json` contains supplementary official references supporting portions of the level design, including NASA skywatching, RSPCA fish welfare, ABA ethical birding, US National Archives genealogy guidance, Arduino tutorials, MediaWiki editing guidance and RHS seed sowing. These supplementary sources are deliberately separate from the four original registered candidate sources. Do not merge them into the central source registry without validating and assigning IDs.
